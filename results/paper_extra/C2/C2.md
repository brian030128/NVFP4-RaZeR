The estimate assumes 8 warps per 128 x 128 CTA and 128 K per steady-state iteration (32 OMMAs = two 64-K blocks of 16 m16n8k64 atoms per warp: 2 x 8 in the 4 x 2 warp arrangement of the stock and wA builds, 8 x 2 in n8k64_wB's 1 x 8); it equals the historical ncu count of the stock kernel (8,388,608, RTX 5090). Every path through the mixed kernels' steady-state iteration issues the same 32 OMMAs as stock, so the E0M3 tags do not change the tensor-pipe instruction count.
